Privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This is a translation of the Spanish original. If the versions differ, the Spanish version prevails.

JourFund is a trading journal to log trades, organize accounts, calculate risk and track goals. This policy explains what information we process when you use the platform.

1. Who we are and contact

The platform is run by the JourFund team, made up of Facundo Benitez and Isaías Kuebler. For privacy questions or requests to access, correct or delete data, write to info@jourfund.com.

2. Data you provide

  • Account data: email address, user ID, display name and profile photo if you add one.
  • Journal information: trading accounts and groups, capital, trades, results, lot size, risk, R:R, notes, screenshots, withdrawals and goals you choose to record.
  • Preferences: appearance, language, selected markets and alert settings.

3. Sign in with Google

If you choose Google, we receive the basic identity data you authorize, such as ID, email, name and photo, to create or identify your account and show your profile. Access is handled through Google and Supabase. We do not receive your Google password and we do not request access to Gmail, contacts or Google Drive files for this sign-in. Data received from Google is used for authentication and your profile; it is not sold or used for personalized advertising.

4. How we use information

We use data to authenticate you, save and sync your journal across devices, calculate statistics and goals, provide tools, send the alerts you set up, resolve issues and protect the service. Calculations depend on the data you record and on available syncing.

5. Storage on your device and in the cloud

The app uses browser storage and cache for sessions, preferences, local copies and the installed app. Synced data is stored with Supabase. Local copies and downloaded backups may remain on your devices until you delete them. Do not delete a copy with pending changes without exporting it if you want to keep them.

6. Notifications

External notifications require your permission. We store the device subscription, preferences, history and the data needed to schedule and deliver alerts. Your browser's or operating system's push services take part in delivery. You can turn off a device from the app or revoke the permission in your browser. Alerts may be visible on the lock screen; set their privacy on your phone. External history is kept for 90 days under the current settings.

7. Providers and external content

Supabase provides authentication, storage and server functions; GitHub Pages serves the app; Google handles sign-in if you choose it. External charts and calendars, including TradingView and Forex Factory, may receive technical information such as IP address, browser or requests when their content opens or loads. If the app fails, we send Sentry a technical error report (message, code file and line, page, language, browser and IP address) so we can fix it; it does not include your trades, amounts, name or email. These providers apply their own policies. Using external services may involve processing data outside Paraguay.

8. Security and access

We use HTTPS connections and access controls to restrict each account's data. The admin team may access information when necessary to operate, maintain and support the service. No system removes all risk; protect your password and devices, and do not upload broker credentials or third-party data in notes or screenshots.

9. Retention and deletion

We keep your synced journal while you keep your account or until we handle a deletion request, except information needed to resolve issues or meet applicable obligations. You can edit and delete records with the available features and export a backup. To delete your account and its server data, write to the contact above from your registered email; we will verify ownership before proceeding. Providers' technical copies may be kept during their backup cycles. Server deletion does not erase backups you downloaded or every local copy on other devices.

10. Your choices

You can request access, correction or deletion of your data, revoke notification permissions and remove Google access from your Google account. Revoking Google does not by itself delete your JourFund journal. For questions or requests contact info@jourfund.com.

11. Changes

We will publish changes on this page with an updated date. Relevant changes to how data is processed will be communicated through the available contact channels or the platform.
